## A. Tile mandate and verdict

**Mandate.** The organs of the crossover-v2 tuning round: *what to play and
where to stand* (`capture_plan`, `sweep_spec`, `programs`, `priors`,
`position_cycle`), *what one take means* (`spatial`, `capture_dispatch`,
`admission`, `feature_classifier`, `delay_landscape`, `commanded`), *what to
prescribe* (`driver_/blend_/alignment_prescription`, `planning`, `proposal`,
`candidates`, `accountability`), *what to persist and grade*
(`durable_state`, `coordinator`, `round_inputs`, `ring_projection`,
`operator_notes`, `frequency_view`), and *the new engine*
(`session`, `session_graph`, `playback_transaction`).

**Verdict: not one system — two session systems and three vocabularies, in one
directory.** The evidence is not architectural taste, it is duplication:

| Fact | Old-flow spelling | New-engine spelling |
|---|---|---|
| pose axis vocabulary | `spatial.py:604,612,615` (own literals) | `contracts.py:1340-1342` (own literals; comment claims spatial is "Owner") |
| a session object | `crossover_v2_flow.CrossoverV2Session` (3,703 LOC, 155 methods) | `session.TuningSession` (442 LOC) |
| a banked take's record | `spatial.TakeClaim` + `*_record` builders | `session._record` (:537-609) |
| a take id | `spatial.take_id_for` | `session._next_take_id` → wraps `take_id_for` |

Both run in **the same request**: `web/correction_crossover_v2.py:6286-6305`
builds a `conductor` (god file), persists it, then constructs a `TuningSession`
whose play seam is bound to `conductor.program_for_phase`. §5's W5-b join has
not landed, so the engine is a *subordinate* of the conductor, not its
replacement.

**Convergence on REFACTOR-CUTOVER §6/§7: real but partial, and slowing at the
class.** `crossover_v2_flow.py` 9,228 → **4,636** (plan target ≈1,500);
`web/correction_crossover_v2.py` 8,088 → **7,165**. But `CrossoverV2Session`
went 6,825→3,703 lines while methods went **158 → 155**: the shrink is prose and
barrels, not organs. Three §6 rows are explicitly *not done at HEAD*: the
constants block (`crossover_v2_flow.py:456-509`, all six re-export doors and the
eight constants still there), `__all__` (`:4582-4630`), and a **fifth,
unnamed barrel** at `:736-806` — four wrappers whose whole job is to call a
`spatial` organ and log the `diagnostics` dict it returned.
