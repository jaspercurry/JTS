# T07 — AEC / mic tile (39 files, 14,635 LOC) @ 2d571e6b8

## A. Mandate & verdict

**Mandate:** own the microphone edge end-to-end — identify the XVF3800, write its volatile
chip profile, resolve/apply the commissioned alignment K, feed jasper-voice's UDP wake legs
(chip-beam passthrough or host AEC3), and publish what happened.

**Verdict: three tangled systems plus two passengers.**
1. *Chip identity + alignment* (`mics/`, `xvf/`, `chip_aec/`, `cli/aec_init`, `cli/aec_commission`,
   `cli/xvf_*`) — coherent, well-layered, the strongest part of the tile.
2. *The bridge* (`cli/aec_bridge*`, `aec_engines/`, `aec_sweep`, `jasper_aec3/`, `enhanced_aec*`)
   — one 708-LOC loop plus five "split" modules, four of which are clean one-way leaves and one
   (`corpus_lanes`) that imports back into the parent and forces a cycle.
3. *The USB-host mic relay* (`usb_mic.py`, `cli/usb_mic.py`) — a self-contained product feature
   with no AEC relationship beyond consuming a bridge leg; the tile's heaviest telemetry.
Passengers: `capture_protocol.py` (belongs to the crossover/measurement tile — see §E) and
`mic_presence.py` (a generic voice-input record that is mic-agnostic by design).

**Earns-its-keep (tried to cut, could not):**
- `xvf_host.COMMANDS` as a closed name-keyed allowlist — it is the mechanism that makes non-negotiable 2 structural rather than a convention. The CLI validates names through `case_insensitive_command`, so there is no numeric resid/cmdid escape hatch; no `ctrl_transfer` exists outside this file; no script issues `--values`. Verified sound.
- The bridge stall ladder (`BridgeStalled` + `_MicStarvationWatchdog` + `Heartbeat`/`WatchdogSec`) — PortAudio's InputStream really is one-shot, and mic disappearance is covered end-to-end: unplug → `99-jasper-aec-reconcile.rules` → reconciler stops **and disables** the bridge and clears `aec-bridge-ready` before its `StartLimitAction=reboot` (4 failures / 300 s) can fire; `tests/test_aec_reconcile.py:814-822` pins that ordering.
- `chip_aec/alignment.py`'s queue-stability rules — `required_queue_samples` / `queue_median_drift` are one rule shared by commissioning and boot, so boot cannot reject the window commissioning accepted.
- Doctor's `_assess_aec_reference_input_from_stats` (`cli/doctor/aec.py:765`) — covers the one silent failure the bridge cannot self-detect (ref thread dead → carry-forward forever, no stall).
- `_process_optional_engine` (`aec_bridge.py:331`) — the deliberate asymmetry (optional legs disable on first failure, primary escapes to systemd) is right.

**Prose:** ~2,670 of 14,530 lines (18%). Worst: `cli/aec_init.py` 392 lines (31%) — e.g. `:441-471` and `:499-527` are ADR-grade essays on clock-step residuals inside docstrings; `mic_presence.py` 131/268 (49%) — `:505-559` module docstring plus `:667-681`; `mics/__init__.py` 34/36 arguing against an abstraction nobody proposed.
