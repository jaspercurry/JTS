## A. Tile mandate and verdict

**Mandate the tile actually serves:** *compile a declared active crossover into a
CamillaDSP graph, prove it is safe to load, apply it, and bank the evidence that
justified it.* Everything in the tile touches one of five jobs: **compile/apply**
(`baseline_profile`, `driver_base_trim`, `tone_plan`), **safety gates**
(`path_safety`, `graph_safety`, `driver_protection`, `excitation_safety_plan`,
`audible_policy`, `volume_latch`), **evidence persistence** (`measurement`,
`bundles`, `commissioning_receipt`, `commissioning_lifecycle`, `round_bank`,
`repeat_floor`, `capture_provenance`, `capture_entry_anchor`,
`seat_level_reference`), **capture orchestration** (`web_measurement`,
`angle_capture`, `playback`, `commissioning_runtime`, `restore_wait`), and
**household-facing view copy** (`crossover_envelope_v2`, `tuning_handoff`).

**Verdict: five coherent systems in one flat bag.** Nothing here is homeless —
every file has real production callers — but the 107-file flat root gives a
newcomer no signal about which of the five they are in. `crossover_envelope_v2.py`
(3,497 lines of household copy) and `baseline_profile.py` (4,177 lines of graph
compilation) are peers in the directory listing. The `crossover_v2/` subpackage
already proves the shape works; the root has simply not followed it.

**Proposed hierarchy** (only the ~30 files I own are placed with confidence;
names are for the synthesis to reconcile with siblings):

| Subpackage | From this tile | Rule for admission |
|---|---|---|
| `safety/` | `path_safety` `graph_safety` `driver_protection` `excitation_safety_plan` `audible_policy` `volume_latch` `driver_safety`\* | pure fail-closed predicates; may not import compile or capture |
| `evidence/` | `measurement` `bundles` `commissioning_receipt` `commissioning_lifecycle` `round_bank` `repeat_floor` `capture_provenance` `capture_entry_anchor` `driver_base_trim` `seat_level_reference` | owns a durable file under `/var/lib/jasper`; one writer named in the header |

| `capture/` | `web_measurement` `angle_capture` `playback` `commissioning_runtime` `restore_wait` | moves the live graph / talks to a device |
| `crossover_v2/` (exists) | `crossover_envelope_v2` → `crossover_v2/envelope.py` | household screen copy; already the home of `refusal_copy.py` |
