# T12-1 — `jasper/audio_measurement/` (half 1 of 2), 23 files / 12,740 LOC

Sibling tile T12-2 owns: `admitted_playback, alignment, analysis, bundles, calibration, excitation,

## A. Tile mandate and verdict

**Mandate:** the hardware-free measurement leaf — compose an excitation program, admit it, play it,
capture it, and turn (program, capture) into typed evidence. It imports nothing upward
(`jasper.{atomic_io, camilla_config_contract, camilla_emit, env_load, json_fields, log_event}` only)
and is consumed by `active_speaker/`, `correction/`, `bass_extension/`, `cli/`, `web/`.

**Verdict: one coherent system with three distinct sub-systems and one passenger.** The three that
belong: (1) *program + admission + playback* (program, excitation_admission, excitation_artifacts,
evidence_identity, playback, correction_lane), (2) *capture* (wired_capture, wired_level_meter,
frame_ledger), (3) *analysis* (program_analysis/*, deconv, distortion, interference_nulls,
gate_disclosure, comparison_bands, room_boundary, mic_identity). The passenger is `delay_graph.py`,
which validates a CamillaDSP graph and contains no measurement. The package boundary is genuinely
well kept — the tangle is *inside* it, in the underscore vocabulary and the four re-rolled
fingerprint helpers.
