# T02 — assistant tools / cues / wake corpus & telemetry (39 files, 16,940 LOC)

## A. Tile mandate & verdict

**Mandate as tiled:** none. This is four unrelated systems stapled together by
the word "wake" and by both being reachable from `voice_daemon`:

1. **LLM tool platform** (`jasper/tools/*`, `tool_catalog_view.py`) — declare, dispatch, catalogue tools. Coherent, the healthiest subsystem here.
2. **Audio cues** (`jasper/cues/*`) — the non-negotiable-6 mechanism. Coherent, small, correct.
3. **Wake research rig** (`wake_corpus/*`, `wake_events.py`, `wake_models.py`, `wake_fusion.py`) — an operator-only corpus recorder + telemetry store. 6,430 LOC, by far the tile's heaviest, and the least finished.
4. **Assorted daemon services** (`usage.py`, `timers.py`, `conversation_history.py`, `assistant_loudness.py`, `assistant_volume.py`, `tts_routing.py`) — four independent SQLite/state stores that happen to be tool-adjacent.

Groups 1 and 2 earn their keep essentially as-is. Group 3 is where the tile's
complexity, dead code and unfinished refactor live. Group 4 is fine
individually but shows one clear cross-cutting duplication (SQLite store
boilerplate, env-bool parsing).

**Extra-focus answers up front:**

- **One dispatch seam?** *Yes, clean.* `dispatch_tool` (tools/__init__.py:766) is the only place `tool.executor.execute()` is awaited in production. No rogue `.fn(...)` calls anywhere in `jasper/`. `voice/trace.py:216` wraps executors (decorator, not a second seam). Gemini and OpenAI/Grok adapters both route through it. §1 of docs/extensibility.md holds.
- **Pack registry consistency?** Mostly good — one ordered `TOOL_PACKS` tuple, per-pack fault isolation with real rollback, `PackOutcome` → `/state` → doctor. Two cracks: three different tool-authoring styles (see F3/F6), and `_CATALOG_HIDDEN` + pack-id derivation spelled differently on the bake side and the read side (F5).
- **wake_corpus after the #4115/#4116 split?** The split moved 4,851 LOC into the package but left `jasper/web/wake_corpus_setup.py` at **1,435 lines**, ~110 of them a re-export facade of 76 names (4 of them private). At least **26 of those 76 re-exports have zero consumers**, and tests already patch `bridge_session` directly (29 sites) rather than through the shim (2 sites). The split is not done: finish it by deleting the facade. `capture_plan.py`'s own `build_capture_plan` is dead on arrival (F2).
- **Cue registry complete & singular?** *One registry, yes* (`cues/registry.py`, 9 CueDefs), with a genuinely good two-sided coverage guard (`tests/test_cue_registry_coverage.py`). But the slugs are spelled twice — as `CueDef(slug=...)` literals and again as `*_CUE_SLUG` constants in 3 other modules — and the guard is a source-text scanner that verifies registry↔play-site *symmetry*, not that every wake-blocking path *has* a cue. NN-6's real gap is unmeasured (F8).
