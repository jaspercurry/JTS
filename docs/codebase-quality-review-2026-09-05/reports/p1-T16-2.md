# T16-web-2 — `jasper/web/` third 2 (14 files, 15,041 LOC) @ 2d571e6b8

## A. Tile mandate & verdict

**Mandate:** stdlib-`http.server` wizard pages behind nginx — parse a request,
render a canonical shell, and hand every real decision to a domain owner.

**Verdict: two tangled systems, not one.** Eleven files honour that mandate and
are in good shape (`system_setup.py` is the reference implementation). The other
three do not: `sound_setup.py` carries a 2,784-LOC active-speaker commissioning
backend that duplicates `jasper/active_speaker/web_commissioning.py`;
`correction_crossover_v2_wired.py` is a measurement-capture driver with no HTTP
in it; `correction_tuning.py` is a paid-LLM spend gate with no HTTP in it.
Strip those three of non-web work and the package becomes one coherent system.

Package-level good news, verified: **zero** inline behaviour `<script>` blocks,
**zero** direct `systemctl` mutations (all go through `restart_broker.manage_units`),
**zero** secrets rendered into HTML or logs, every subprocess spawn on the
`correction_lane` seam, every peer HTTP call timeout-bounded and SSRF-guarded.
