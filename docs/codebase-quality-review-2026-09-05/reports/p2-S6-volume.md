# S6-volume — every way the listening level changes, and every way it is restored. SHA 2d571e6b8

## A. Verdict

The **arbitration** is right and the **ownership** is not. `VolumeOwner` + `volume_latch` are the
best-built pair in this scenario: four ranked claims, one confirm tolerance, one write door behind
`_coerce_main_volume_db`, fail-closed acquires, an unwinding `finally`. But that owner arbitrates
*within one process*, and the level is written from **four** processes (jasper-voice, jasper-control,
jasper-mux, jasper-web) — two of which construct a **second** `VolumeOwner` beside the one they
registered, so the invariant its docstring calls "the arbitration failure this whole wave deletes" is
false in the two processes that carry the most writes. Underneath that, the **canonical level (0-100)
has exactly one owner** (`speaker_volume.json` via `VolumeState`) and that half is clean. The clamp
is intact at every emitter and at the durable apply path, with one unguarded live door
(`set_active_config_raw`) that the doctor also cannot see, because the doctor reads the statefile's
file and not the running graph. The worst structural fact is not in any of the volume modules: **boot
restore, the drift reconciler, and all inbound Spotify/BT observation live only inside jasper-voice,
which the streambox profile disables** — one shipped profile has no boot restore and no self-healing
at all. Two smaller things are outright bugs: 1% listening level silently mutes and then re-mutes at
1 Hz forever, and a camilla-master→push handoff clears its guard on a 0.75 s timer against a Spotify
*cloud* round trip the same file documents as "200-800 ms typical".
