## A. Mandate and verdict

**Mandate:** the crossover-v2 tuning domain — the vocabulary, the grading, the
prescriptions, the durable record, and the offline instruments that read a
banked round — with `crossover_v2_flow.py` (host) and `jasper/web/` (front end)
kept out by construction.

**Verdict: four systems in one flat directory of 64 modules.** They do not
import each other in a cycle and the layering is honest, but nothing in the
hierarchy tells a newcomer which of the four a file belongs to:

| System | Files in this tile |
|---|---|
| **Engine** (doors-and-banks) | `session_seams`, `record_store`, `record_index`, `volume_claim`, `composition`, `program_transaction`, `measure_spec`, `measurement_phase`, `door`, `capture_source` |
| **Conductor** (old flow's helpers) | `journey`, `refusal_copy`, `conductor_context`, `delta_probe_run`, `verification`, `round_evidence`, `intervention`, `plan_assembly`, `contracts` |
| **Offline instruments** | `evidence_packet`, `round_views`, `harmonic_evidence`, `gate_sweep`, `close_reference`, `round_captures`, `forward_model` |
| **Prescriptions** | `topology_prescription`, `prescription_spool`, `blend_correction`, `feature_classification`, `handoff_doors`, `fc_sweep` |

Convergence on REFACTOR-CUTOVER §6 is **real but partial**:
`crossover_v2_flow.py` is 4,636 lines (§6 baseline 9,228), the delta probe, the
VERIFY tolerance derivation and the SNR log-field helpers have landed here, and
§6.2's FOLD ruling is implemented — `record_store._ROUTES` carries all six
artifact kinds on one `bank`, `EngineSeams` still has no sixth seam. What has
**not** landed: §6.1 (all eight constants still at `crossover_v2_flow.py:458-502`,
no ADR written), and `web/correction_crossover_v2.py` is 7,165 lines with its

will mislead an executor: it cites `EngineSeams` as five fields at
`session_seams.py:299-303`; at HEAD the file is 133 lines and the dataclass has
four (`graph, volume, records, play`) — `recommend` does not exist.
