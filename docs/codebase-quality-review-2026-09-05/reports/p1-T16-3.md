# T16-web-3 — stdlib-HTTP wizard pages (14 files, 15,069 LOC)

## A. Tile mandate and verdict

**Mandate:** serve the LAN management pages behind nginx as stdlib
`ThreadingHTTPServer`s — parse a request, validate a body, call a domain owner,
render or answer JSON.

**Verdict: three tangled systems, not one.** (1) Six genuine wizard pages
(`voice`, `wifi`, `bluetooth`, `wake`, `chat`) that mostly do the mandate well.
(2) `correction_setup.py` — a 5,127-LOC **six-page multiplexer** (54 routes across
`/`, `/room`, `/crossover`, `/measurements`, `/bass`, `/sync`) that also owns the
room-correction domain, the process-global capture slot, an asyncio bridge, the
mic-calibration paths, and the tuning-LLM spend gate. (3) Two files that are not
web at all (`correction_crossover_backend.py`, `volume_floor_tone.py`): pure
domain owners with zero HTTP, parked in `jasper/web/` because that is where their
single caller lives.

The hierarchy does not tell a newcomer where things live: nothing in the name
`correction_setup` says it serves `/sound/measurements/` and `/sync/`, and
`correction_crossover_backend` is named for the web page it feeds rather than the
`active_speaker` domain it implements.

## D. If I could do one thing to this tile

Turn `correction_setup._make_handler` inside out: move `cfg` onto the server object,
make `Handler` a module-level class, and replace the 16 hand-written exception
ladders in `do_POST` with one route table. That single change deletes ~450 lines,
un-indents 1,000, makes the handler importable and testable, and retires the two
source-text tests whose own comments say the closure is why they exist. It is the
prerequisite for every other split of that file — and it is the Phase D row the
ledger is missing.
